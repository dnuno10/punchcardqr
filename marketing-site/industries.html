Industry templates

A loyalty card template for your kind of business

Every template below sets sensible starting colors, a stamp icon and a default reward — pick one to launch faster, then customize anything you like from your dashboard.

Coffee shops

Stamp card with a coffee-cup icon. Default reward: a free coffee after enough stamps — the classic done right, digitally.

Restaurants

Visit-based card that rewards a free appetizer or dish — built to bring regulars back on a weekly cadence.

Bakeries & pastry shops

Gift-icon stamp card tuned for frequent small purchases, with a free pastry as the default reward.

Bars & nightlife

Visit-based rewards for happy-hour regulars, with a free drink as the default milestone.

Salons & spas

Scissor-icon card built around appointments, rewarding a free service after a set number of visits.

Gyms & fitness studios

Dumbbell-icon card that rewards class attendance streaks with a free class instead of chasing renewals.

Retail stores

Purchase-based card built for a straightforward "20% off your next order" style reward.

Car washes & auto services

Wash-based card that rewards a free wash after a set number of visits — built for quick, repeat transactions.

Pet services & grooming

Paw-icon card for grooming and boarding businesses, with a free grooming session as the default reward.

Professional services

A visit-based card for recurring consultations, offering a free consultation as the milestone reward.

Healthcare & wellness

A leaf-icon card built for recurring visits, with a free session as the default reward for consistency.

Anything else

Start from the "Other" template — a neutral blue design with a plain stamp — and customize every color, icon and rule yourself.

Don't see an exact match? That's fine.

Every template is just a starting point — colors, the stamp icon, punch count and reward name are yours to change any time.
