How it works

Three steps, no terminal, no plastic cards to reorder

Everything runs on the phone your customer is already holding.

Build your card in minutes

Pick an industry template, set how many stamps earn a reward, and choose your colors. Your card is live with its own join link and QR code right away.

Customers join by scanning

Print your join QR at the counter or share the link online. One scan, no app store, no account password — the card lives on their phone's browser.

You scan, they earn

At checkout, scan the customer's card with your phone or tablet camera. Punches, rewards and redemptions update instantly for both of you.

Built for the counter, not a boardroom

Everything a loyalty program needs — nothing it doesn't

QR scan-to-punch

Your camera is the terminal. No hardware to buy, no card reader to maintain — punches and redemptions happen from any phone or tablet.

Custom rewards & signup bonuses

Set how many punches earn a reward, offer a welcome bonus to new members, and pick the stamp icon and colors that fit your brand.

Live dashboard & analytics

Track punches, active customers, repeat rate and reward redemptions from one dashboard — and export the raw data as CSV whenever you need it.

Promotions & double-punch days

Run a Tuesday double-punch promotion or a limited-time bonus without touching a single customer's card by hand.

Multi-location support

Running more than one shop? Add locations and let staff pick which one they're scanning from — everything still rolls up into one dashboard.

Birthday & win-back emails

Automatically reward birthdays with bonus stamps and re-engage customers who haven't visited in a while — set it once, it runs itself.
